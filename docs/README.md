# Kite documentation

Kite is a self-custody interface for tokenized equities on Solana. It combines live issuer-listed market discovery, thematic baskets, paper investing, wallet-approved swaps, and recurring investment plans.

Kite is a non-custodial interface, not a broker, custodian, investment adviser, or issuer of tokenized stocks. Availability, trading status, liquidity, and legal eligibility depend on the relevant issuer, router, wallet, and jurisdiction.
