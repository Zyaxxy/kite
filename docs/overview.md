# Kite: investing in ideas, directly from your wallet

Kite makes tokenized equities on Solana easier to discover, understand, and use. Instead of navigating a long list of isolated assets, users can explore companies, compare market data, and buy a single stock or a transparent thematic basket such as AI, semiconductors, or mega-cap technology.

## The problem

Tokenized equities bring familiar company exposure onchain, but the experience is fragmented. Users have to find the right asset, understand what its token represents, manage wallet balances, assemble a theme manually, and remember to invest again later.

Kite turns those separate tasks into one guided flow:

1. Discover issuer-listed tokenized equities and curated themes.
2. Understand an asset through company context, price data, fundamentals, technical history, and sourced news where available.
3. Choose Paper mode for a risk-free product demo or Actual mode for a wallet-approved onchain trade.
4. Set up a bounded recurring plan for a stock or basket when regular investing is the goal.

## What makes Kite different

- **Self-custody:** assets and signing authority remain with the user's Solana wallet.
- **Transparent baskets:** every basket exposes its constituents and target allocation rather than hiding them behind a synthetic product.
- **Evidence-led market information:** missing, stale, or unavailable data stays visible; Kite does not manufacture prices, sentiment, history, or fills.
- **One review for a basket:** compatible basket routes can be composed into one wallet-approved transaction.
- **Web and mobile:** the same typed SDK and API contracts support both experiences.

Kite is designed as a clear access and orchestration layer around issuer-listed tokens, Solana wallets, and Jupiter routing. It does not create a Kite vault, mint replacement stock tokens, or hold a server wallet for user trades.
